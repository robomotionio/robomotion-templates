# Vantage Labs — Data Protection Policy

Version 3.1 · Owner: Legal · Aligned to GDPR

## 1. Principles

Personal data is processed lawfully, fairly and transparently; collected for specified
purposes; limited to what is necessary; kept accurate; retained no longer than needed;
and kept secure. Every processing activity must satisfy all six.

## 2. Lawful basis

Every processing activity has a recorded lawful basis: consent, contract, legal
obligation, vital interests, public task or legitimate interests. The basis is recorded
in the processing register before processing begins, not afterwards.

Consent must be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous, and must be as easy
to withdraw as to give. Pre-ticked boxes are not consent.

## 3. Data subject requests

A data subject request must be acknowledged within 3 working days and answered within
**one calendar month** of receipt. The month runs from receipt, not from the point the
request reaches the right team, so requests are forwarded to Legal the day they arrive.

The period may be extended by two further months for complex requests, but the data
subject must be told of the extension within the original month.

Requests arrive in any form and by any channel. A request in a support ticket or a
social media message is still a request.

## 4. Retention

Personal data is retained per the retention schedule maintained by Legal. Where no
entry exists, the default is seven years for records with a financial or contractual
dimension and two years otherwise.

Candidate data for unsuccessful applicants is retained for 12 months.

## 5. Breaches

A personal data breach must be reported to Legal **immediately and in any event within
24 hours** of discovery. Legal assesses whether the breach is notifiable to the
supervisory authority, which must happen within **72 hours** of the company becoming
aware.

An employee who discovers a breach reports it and does not attempt to assess severity
themselves. Under-reporting is the risk, not over-reporting.

## 6. Transfers

Personal data may be transferred outside the EEA only under an adequacy decision,
standard contractual clauses, or another approved safeguard, recorded before transfer.

## 7. Processors

Every processor is engaged under a written data processing agreement before any data is
shared. Sub-processors require prior written authorisation.

## 8. Privacy by design

New systems and material changes to existing ones require a data protection impact
assessment where processing is likely to be high risk, completed before build starts.
