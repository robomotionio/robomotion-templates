# Vantage Labs — Expense Policy

Version 2026.1 · Owner: Finance Operations

## 1. Filing deadlines

**Expense claims must be filed within 30 days of the expenditure.** Claims filed after
30 days require a written explanation and line manager approval. **Claims filed after 60
days will not be reimbursed** — this limit is absolute and Finance cannot waive it.

Claims are paid in the payroll run following approval. The cut-off for a given payroll
run is the 20th of the month.

## 2. Receipts and evidence

Any single expense line above **EUR 250 requires an itemised receipt**; a card statement
alone is not accepted. Below EUR 250 a card statement is sufficient where an itemised
receipt is genuinely unavailable, and the claim must say why.

An itemised receipt shows the merchant, the date, the individual items and the tax. A
credit-card slip showing only a total is not an itemised receipt.

Receipts in a foreign currency are converted at the card provider's rate where the
corporate card was used, or at the ECB reference rate on the date of spend otherwise.

## 3. Policy EXP-114 — Client entertainment

**Policy EXP-114 governs client entertainment.** The host must record every attendee by
name and organisation, and the business purpose, on the claim. A claim naming only
"client dinner" will be returned.

Entertainment is capped at EUR 90 per attendee including drinks and service. Alcohol is
reimbursable as part of a meal and is not reimbursable on its own.

Entertainment of public officials is prohibited without prior written clearance from
Legal, without exception and regardless of value.

Where entertainment is claimed for a day, per diem for that day is forfeited — see the
Travel Policy.

## 4. Policy EXP-118 — Home office equipment

**Policy EXP-118 covers home office equipment.** Employees may claim up to **EUR 500 once
every 24 months** against **form F-2201**. The allowance covers a desk, a chair, a
monitor, a keyboard and a mouse.

Equipment bought under EXP-118 remains company property and must be returned or bought
out at book value when the employee leaves.

Laptops, phones and anything that stores company data are not claimable under EXP-118 —
they are issued by IT and are governed by the Information Security Standard.

## 5. Non-reimbursable

The following are never reimbursable: traffic and parking fines; personal grooming;
in-room entertainment; loyalty scheme fees; personal travel insurance; gifts to
employees; and any expense already covered by per diem.

## 6. Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued at manager request to employees who travel more than four
times a year. Personal spend on a corporate card must be repaid within 14 days.

A lost corporate card must be reported to Finance and the card provider immediately.
