# Vantage Labs — IT Acceptable Use Policy

Version 4.0 · Owner: IT

## 1. Scope

This policy covers company devices, company accounts and any personal device used to
access company systems. It sits under the Information Security Standard.

## 2. Acceptable use

Company systems are provided for work. Reasonable personal use is permitted where it
does not interfere with work, consume significant resources, or breach any other policy.

## 3. Prohibited

The following are prohibited: sharing account credentials; circumventing security
controls; installing unlicensed software; connecting unapproved devices to the corporate
network; using company systems to run a personal business; and accessing, storing or
distributing unlawful material.

## 4. Software installation

Software is installed from the managed catalogue. Anything outside the catalogue
requires an IT request and, where it processes company data, a security review.

Browser extensions are software. An extension with access to page content can read
everything the employee can, and is reviewed on the same basis.

## 5. AI assistants

AI assistants may be used for work only where the tool appears on the approved list
maintained by Security. Confidential and Restricted data must never be pasted into a
tool that is not on that list, including free consumer tiers of otherwise approved tools.

Output from an AI assistant is checked before it is relied on or sent externally. The
employee remains accountable for the work.

## 6. Email and messaging

Company email is for company business. Auto-forwarding company email to a personal
account is prohibited.

Phishing is reported using the report button rather than deleted, so that IT can find
other recipients. Nobody is penalised for reporting something that turns out to be
genuine.

## 7. Monitoring

The company monitors systems for security and availability. Monitoring is proportionate,
and content is accessed only where there is a specific, documented reason and with
People Team involvement.

## 8. Leaving

Company data is returned and company accounts are closed on the last working day.
Copying company data to personal storage at any point is a disciplinary matter.
