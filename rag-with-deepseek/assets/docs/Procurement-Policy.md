# Vantage Labs — Procurement Policy

Version 2.4 · Owner: Finance Operations

## 1. When this applies

This policy applies to any commitment of company money to a third party that is not
travel or an expense claim: software, hardware, services, contractors and subscriptions.
Travel is governed by the Travel Policy and reimbursement by the Expense Policy.

## 2. Approval thresholds

Spend is approved against the annual committed value, not the monthly instalment. A
EUR 400 per month subscription is a EUR 4,800 commitment and is approved at that level.

- Up to EUR 1,000: line manager.
- EUR 1,000 to EUR 10,000: department head.
- EUR 10,000 to EUR 50,000: Finance Director.
- Above EUR 50,000: two directors, one of whom must be the CFO.

Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is a disciplinary matter.

## 3. Competitive quotes

Purchases above EUR 10,000 require three written quotes, or a documented single-source
justification where three suppliers do not exist. Quotes must be comparable in scope.

## 4. Contracts

No employee may sign a contract on behalf of the company without written delegated
authority. Delegated authority is held by directors and by named signatories listed in
the delegation register maintained by Legal.

Any contract with an auto-renewal clause must be registered with Finance, with the
notice date recorded, at the point of signature.

## 5. Suppliers

New suppliers are onboarded by Finance and require a completed supplier form, bank
details verified by callback to a number obtained independently of the invoice, and a
sanctions screening check.

Bank detail changes on an existing supplier are verified by callback in every case,
without exception. Email confirmation alone is never sufficient — this is the single
most common route for invoice fraud.

## 6. Software and data

Any tool that will process company data requires a security review before purchase, and
any tool processing personal data additionally requires a data protection assessment.
See the Information Security Standard and the Data Protection Policy.

Purchasing a tool on a personal card and claiming it back is not a route around this
policy, and such claims are refused.

## 7. Purchase orders

A purchase order is raised before the commitment, not after the invoice arrives. An
invoice received without a matching purchase order is held until one is raised and
approved, which delays payment to the supplier.
