# Vantage Labs — Travel Policy

Version 4.1 · Owner: Finance Operations · Applies to all employees and contractors

## 1. Scope

This policy covers travel booked on company funds, whether paid by corporate card,
personal card or invoice. It sits alongside the Expense Policy, which governs how
money already spent is claimed back.

## 2. Booking and approval

All travel must be booked through the company travel desk. Bookings made outside the
travel desk are reimbursed only where the traveller can show the fare was cheaper and
the booking was made at least seven days before departure.

Domestic travel needs no separate approval. International travel requires written
approval from the traveller's line manager before booking.

## 3. Air travel

Economy class is the default for all flights under six hours. Business class requires
prior written approval from a director, and the approval must be attached to the claim.

Flights of more than nine hours may be booked in premium economy without director
approval where the traveller is expected to work on the day of arrival.

Seat selection and one item of hold baggage are reimbursable. Lounge access, priority
boarding and travel insurance bought at the gate are not.

## 4. Accommodation

Hotel spend is capped at **EUR 180 per night in tier-1 cities** and **EUR 120 per night
elsewhere**. The cap is on room rate including local tax, and excludes breakfast where
breakfast is billed separately.

Tier-1 cities for the purposes of this policy are Amsterdam, **Berlin**, Copenhagen,
Dublin, Frankfurt, Geneva, London, Munich, Paris, Stockholm, Vienna and Zurich.

Where no room is available inside the cap, the traveller may book the cheapest available
room and attach a short written justification to the claim. Overages without a
justification are not reimbursed.

## 5. Meals and incidentals

Per diem for international travel is **EUR 65 per day**, covering meals and incidentals.
Receipts are not required for per diem, and per diem is not reduced by meals provided
by a hotel or a conference.

Per diem is paid at half rate for the day of departure and the day of return.

Per diem and itemised meal claims cannot both be made for the same day. A traveller
hosting clients should claim the meal as entertainment under the Expense Policy and
forgo per diem for that day.

## 6. Ground transport

Rail is the default for journeys under four hours where a direct service exists.
Standard class on domestic rail, second class on international rail.

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursable between airport or station and hotel or office,
and for any journey after 22:00. Car hire requires line manager approval and is limited
to compact class.

Mileage in a private vehicle is reimbursed at EUR 0.38 per kilometre.

## 7. Duty of care

**All international travel must be registered with the duty-of-care system at least five
working days before departure.** Registration records the itinerary, accommodation and
an emergency contact. Travel that has not been registered is not covered by the company
travel insurance policy, and the traveller carries the risk personally.

Travel to a country rated High or Extreme by the company's risk provider additionally
requires sign-off from the Head of Security, regardless of trip length.

## 8. Combining business and personal travel

Personal travel may be combined with a business trip where it does not increase the
cost to the company. The traveller must obtain a quote for the business-only itinerary
from the travel desk before booking, and claim no more than that amount.

Accommodation and per diem are not payable for personal days.
