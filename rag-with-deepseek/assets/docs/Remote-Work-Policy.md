# Vantage Labs — Remote Work Policy

Version 3.0 · Owner: People Team

## 1. Hybrid working

Remote work is available **up to 3 days per week** by default. Teams may agree a
different pattern where the work requires it, recorded with the People Team.

A **fully remote arrangement requires a signed variation to the employment contract**,
approved by the department head. Fully remote employees are expected on site for
quarterly team weeks, and travel for those is covered by the Travel Policy.

## 2. Working from another country

Working from a country other than the country of employment is permitted for up to 20
working days per calendar year, and requires notice to the People Team before travel
because of tax and social security implications.

Working from a country where the company has no legal entity for more than 20 days is
not permitted under any circumstances.

## 3. Home office

The company contributes **EUR 30 per month toward home internet** for employees working
remotely two or more days per week. The contribution is paid through payroll and needs
no claim.

Home office equipment is claimed separately under policy EXP-118 in the Expense Policy.
The internet contribution and the equipment allowance are independent of each other.

## 4. Availability

Remote employees keep the same core hours as on-site employees and are reachable on the
company's messaging platform during them. Calendars must reflect actual availability.

## 5. Equipment and security

Company equipment used at home is subject to the Information Security Standard in full.
Personal devices may not be used to access production systems.
