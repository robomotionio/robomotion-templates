# Vantage Labs — Code of Conduct

Version 2026 · Owner: People Team and Legal

## 1. How we work

Everyone is entitled to a workplace free of harassment, discrimination and bullying.
This applies at the office, at remote work, at client sites, at conferences and in every
company channel online.

## 2. Discrimination and harassment

Discrimination on the basis of any protected characteristic is prohibited. Harassment is
unwanted conduct that violates dignity or creates an intimidating, hostile, degrading or
offensive environment, and it is judged by its effect, not by the intent behind it.

Reports are taken seriously, investigated promptly and handled as confidentially as the
investigation allows. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is itself a
serious disciplinary matter.

## 3. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest is declared as soon as it is recognised, to the line manager and
to Legal. Common cases are outside employment, a financial interest in a supplier or
competitor, and a close personal relationship in a reporting line.

Declaring a conflict is not an admission of wrongdoing. Failing to declare one is.

## 4. Gifts and hospitality

Gifts and hospitality may be accepted where they are modest, infrequent and could not
reasonably be seen to influence a decision. Anything above **EUR 100 in value must be
declared** in the gifts register.

Cash and cash equivalents are never acceptable, at any value.

Gifts to public officials require prior written clearance from Legal, without exception.

## 5. Anti-bribery

The company prohibits bribery in every form, including facilitation payments. This
applies to employees, contractors and anyone acting on the company's behalf.

Where a payment is demanded under threat to personal safety, the employee's safety comes
first: pay, leave, and report it to Legal at the first opportunity.

## 6. Confidential information

Company and client confidential information is not discussed in public places, on public
transport, or in any forum where it could be overheard, and is not shared outside the
company without a confidentiality agreement in place.

## 7. Speaking up

Concerns may be raised with a line manager, the People Team, Legal, or through the
anonymous reporting line, which is operated by an independent third party and is
available in every country the company operates in.
