# Vantage Labs — Health and Safety Policy

Version 1.8 · Owner: Facilities

## 1. Commitment

The company provides a safe place of work and expects everyone to take reasonable care
of their own safety and that of others.

## 2. Reporting

Accidents, injuries and near misses are recorded in the accident book and reported to
Facilities **within 24 hours**. Near misses matter as much as accidents: they are the
cheapest possible warning.

Serious injuries are reported immediately and the scene left undisturbed where it is
safe to do so.

## 3. First aid and fire

Trained first aiders and fire marshals are listed on the notice board on every floor and
on the intranet. On hearing the alarm, leave by the nearest exit, do not use the lifts,
and go to the assembly point.

Evacuation drills are held twice a year and participation is not optional.

## 4. Display screen equipment

Every employee whose work involves prolonged screen use is entitled to a workstation
assessment, on site or at home, and to an eye test paid by the company every two years.

Where the assessment recommends specific equipment, it is provided by the company and is
not charged against the EXP-118 home office allowance.

## 5. Working at home

The employee is responsible for a safe home working environment. The company provides a
self-assessment checklist on the intranet, to be completed on starting home working and
whenever the setup changes materially.

## 6. Lone working

Lone working at client sites or outside normal hours requires the line manager to know
the location and expected finish time, and a check-in on completion.

## 7. Wellbeing

The employee assistance programme is free, confidential, available 24 hours a day, and
open to the employee's immediate family. The company is not told who uses it.

## 8. Stress

Workload concerns are a health and safety matter, not only a management one, and may be
raised with the line manager, the People Team, or through the employee assistance
programme.
