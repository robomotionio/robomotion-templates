# Vantage Labs — Employee Handbook

2026 edition · Owner: People Team

## 1. Working hours

Standard hours are 40 per week. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time; outside core
hours employees arrange their own schedule with their team.

## 2. Annual leave

Employees accrue **2.08 days of paid leave per completed month of service**, to a maximum
of **25 days per calendar year**. Accrual begins on the first day of employment and part
months are not counted.

Unused leave may be carried into the following year up to a ceiling of **5 days**, and
carried-over leave must be taken **before 31 March** or it lapses. Leave is never paid
out in lieu except on termination.

Requests for leave of **more than 5 consecutive days require approval from the department
head at least 14 days in advance.** Shorter requests need line manager approval only.

Public holidays follow the employee's country of employment and do not count against
the annual allowance.

## 3. Sick leave

Up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year require no medical certificate. Absence beyond
three consecutive days requires a certificate regardless of the annual total.

## 4. Parental leave

Parental leave follows statutory entitlement in the country of employment, topped up to
full pay for the first 16 weeks. Notice of at least 8 weeks is expected where the date
is known in advance.

## 5. Probation and notice

Probation is six months. Notice during probation is two weeks on either side. After
probation, notice is one month for employees and two months for senior roles.

## 6. Performance and pay review

Pay is reviewed once a year, effective 1 April. Promotions may take effect at any point
in the year and are not restricted to the review cycle.

## 7. Grievances

Concerns should be raised with the line manager first. Where that is not appropriate,
they may be raised directly with the People Team or through the anonymous reporting
line. Retaliation for raising a concern in good faith is a disciplinary matter.
