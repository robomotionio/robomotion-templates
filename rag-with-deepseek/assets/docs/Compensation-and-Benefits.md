# Vantage Labs — Compensation and Benefits

2026 edition · Owner: People Team

## 1. Pay

Salaries are paid monthly, on the **last working day of the month**, in the employee's
local currency. Payslips are available in the HR system from two working days before
payment.

Pay is reviewed annually with effect from **1 April**. The review considers role, market
data and individual contribution. A promotion may take effect at any time.

## 2. Bonus

The company bonus is discretionary and paid annually in March, based on company
performance and individual contribution. An employee must be employed and not under
notice on the payment date to receive it.

New joiners are eligible on a pro-rata basis from their start date.

## 3. Pension

The company contributes 6% of base salary to the pension scheme where the employee
contributes 3% or more, and 4% otherwise. Contributions begin after successful
completion of probation.

## 4. Health

Private medical cover is provided for the employee from the start date, and may be
extended to a partner and children at the employee's cost through payroll.

An annual health check is available to all employees from age 40, or earlier on
occupational health advice.

## 5. Learning

Each employee has a **learning budget of EUR 1,500 per calendar year** for courses,
conferences and books relevant to their role. It is approved by the line manager and
does not carry over.

Conference travel is booked under the Travel Policy and comes out of the travel budget,
not the learning budget. Only the ticket itself is charged to learning.

Professional membership fees relevant to the role are covered separately and do not use
the learning budget.

## 6. Referrals

A referral bonus of EUR 2,000 is paid where a referred candidate is hired and completes
probation. It is paid in the payroll run following the probation date.

## 7. Equipment

Laptops are refreshed every three years, or sooner where the device no longer meets the
role's needs. Home office equipment is claimed under policy EXP-118 in the Expense
Policy; the two are separate and neither reduces the other.

## 8. Leaving

Final pay includes salary to the last working day and payment for accrued but untaken
leave. Overpaid leave taken in advance is deducted.
