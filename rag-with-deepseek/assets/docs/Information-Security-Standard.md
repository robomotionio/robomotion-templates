# Vantage Labs — Information Security Standard

Version 5.2 · Owner: Security · Aligned to ISO/IEC 27001

## 1. Devices

All laptops must use full-disk encryption, enabled by IT at issue and not to be disabled.
Screen lock must engage after no more than five minutes.

**A lost or stolen device must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours**, by phone if
the reporter has no working device. Reporting a loss promptly is never a disciplinary
matter; failing to report one is.

## 2. Access

Access follows least privilege and is reviewed quarterly. Production access requires a
named business justification and expires after 90 days unless renewed.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every company system that supports it.
SMS is not an acceptable second factor for production systems.

## 3. Credentials

Credentials are stored in the company password manager and nowhere else. Credentials
must never appear in source code, in a ticket, in a chat message or in a document.

Shared accounts are prohibited except where a system cannot support individual accounts,
and each exception is registered with Security and reviewed annually.

## 4. Data classification

Data is classified Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and
Restricted data may not leave company-managed systems, and may not be pasted into
third-party tools including AI assistants that are not on the approved list.

The approved-tools list is maintained by Security and published on the intranet.

## 5. Training

**New joiners must complete security awareness training within their first 10 working
days.** Access to production systems is withheld until the training is signed off.
All employees repeat the training annually.

## 6. Incidents

A suspected incident is reported to IT Security immediately. Employees should not
investigate on their own, and should not delete anything.

## 7. Travel

Devices taken to a country rated High or Extreme by the company's risk provider must be
loan devices issued by IT, not the employee's normal laptop. See the Travel Policy for
the approval that trip itself requires.
