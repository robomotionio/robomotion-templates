# Kestrel M2 — Service and Warranty

Revision 4.2

## What is covered

Every Kestrel M2 carries a 24-month warranty from the date on the invoice,
covering the mainboard, the power supply, the stepper motors, the linear rails,
the touchscreen and the frame. The warranty is with the machine and transfers if
you sell it; keep the invoice.

## What is not covered

The following are consumables and are excluded from the warranty regardless of
how soon they fail:

- nozzles, of any material
- the build plate and its PEI coating
- belts and idler pulleys
- the PTFE liner in the hot end
- filament, glue and adhesives

Damage from a modified firmware, a third-party hot end, or printing a material
above 280 °C is also excluded.

## Raising a service request

1. Note the serial number. It is on a silver label on the back of the frame,
   below the power inlet, and starts with KM2.
2. Note the error code on the panel, if there is one.
3. Open a request at support.kestrel3d.example and attach a photo of the failed
   print and a photo of the label.

We answer service requests within one working day. If a part has to be replaced
under warranty we ship it within three working days and pay the postage both
ways.

## Turnaround

A printer sent in for repair is back with you inside ten working days. Out of
warranty, the workshop rate is 60 euros per hour with a one-hour minimum, quoted
before any work starts.
